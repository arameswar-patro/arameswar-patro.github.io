A curated list of websites, databases, and blogs that are invaluable for researchers in algebra, geometry, and beyond.

The Stacks Project

An incredible, open-source repository of algebraic geometry. It rigorously builds up the foundations of scheme theory, algebraic spaces, and stacks.

nLab

A wiki-driven project focused on mathematics, physics, and philosophy from the perspective of higher category theory and homotopy theory. Great for looking up structural properties of algebraic groups.

MathOverflow

A question-and-answer site specifically dedicated to research-level mathematics. The discussions here often provide insights you won't find in textbooks.

arXiv: Mathematics

The standard repository for electronic preprints (e-prints) of research papers in mathematics. Essential for keeping up with the absolute latest developments in the field.

What's New (Terence Tao's Blog)

Updates on Terence Tao's research, as well as brilliant, accessible expositions of deep mathematical concepts and advice for young researchers.

Quanta Magazine: Mathematics

Excellent high-level journalism that covers breakthroughs in mathematics. It's a great way to stay informed about what's happening outside your specific subfield.

Mathematical Resources

A curated list of websites, databases, and blogs that are invaluable.

The Stacks Project

An incredible, open-source repository of algebraic geometry. It rigorously builds up the foundations of scheme theory, algebraic spaces, and stacks.

nLab

A wiki-driven project focused on mathematics, physics, and philosophy from the perspective of higher category theory and homotopy theory.

MathOverflow

A question-and-answer site specifically dedicated to research-level mathematics. The discussions here often provide insights you won't find in textbooks.

arXiv: Mathematics

The standard repository for electronic preprints (e-prints) of research papers in mathematics. Essential for keeping up with the absolute latest developments in the field.

What's New (Terence Tao's Blog)

Updates on Terence Tao's research, as well as brilliant, accessible expositions of deep mathematical concepts and advice for young researchers.

Quanta Magazine: Mathematics

Excellent high-level journalism that covers breakthroughs in mathematics. It's a great way to stay informed about what's happening outside your specific subfield.
